How recording works

Walk the route with your phone. The app draws your path on the map by itself — you just tap the buttons below to mark the important spots.

Tour picker (top bar)

“New tour” records a fresh route. Pick a saved tour instead to edit it — add POIs or extend its path.

Record / Stop

Start walking your route (a new tour asks for a name first). Tap Stop when you're done to save it.

Waypoint

Mark a spot the route must pass through — like a turn or a junction.

1POI

A primary point of interest — a main stop. Give it a name (and optionally a category) — the briefing visitors hear is written later in the dashboard.

2POI

A secondary point of interest — something worth mentioning along the way, not a main stop.

Undo

Remove the last waypoint or POI you added, in case of a mistake.

Resume unsaved route?

We found a route you were recording that was never saved. You can bring it back, or discard it and start fresh.
